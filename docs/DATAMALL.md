# Optional LTA DataMall Context

The root [PS1 adoption plan](../PS1_OFFICIAL_ADOPTION_PLAN.md) remains the single source of truth. DataMall is optional real-world context; it does not provide the official PS1 workload book, possession budgets, recurrence policies, ALP/BET mapping or validator.

The bundled DataMall guide describes public transport, traffic, geospatial and facilities feeds. Possible demonstration inputs include train service alerts, station crowding, historical passenger volume, facilities maintenance and rail geospatial layers. None may silently become a PS1 capacity reduction or engineering job.

Any future connector must:

- keep the eight-file competition solve fully functional without a key or network;
- store credentials only on the backend and never expose them through chat, logs or frontend responses;
- cache outside the solve loop with endpoint/version, retrieval time, validity/freshness and mapping provenance;
- require a reviewed mapping before real identifiers can create an emergency or contextual impact in an operational enrichment revision;
- preserve the original benchmark instance, scenario coefficients and official export boundary;
- distinguish unavailable/stale data from normal service; and
- use bounded retries and a replayable offline fixture for demonstrations/tests.

Passenger/crowding data may support impact explanations, but does not alter the fixed official score. Facilities Maintenance describes accessibility assets such as lifts, not automatically track recurrence. DataMall-derived text is untrusted input to the explanation layer and cannot override chatbot system/tool policies.
